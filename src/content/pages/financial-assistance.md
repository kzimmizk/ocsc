description: "Registration discounts for OCSC families who qualify for free or reduced-price school meals."
---

Ouray County Soccer Club wants every child to have the opportunity to play.
Subject to available club funding:

- Players who qualify for **reduced-price school meals** may receive a **50%
  registration discount**.
- Players who qualify for **free school meals** may receive a **100%
  registration discount**.

Eligibility is based on the current Colorado free and reduced-price school meal
guidelines.

## Proof of Approval

**Proof of approval is required during soccer registration.** Please provide a
screenshot or copy of the second page of your school meal application showing
its approved status. An application that has not yet been approved cannot be
used as proof of eligibility.

The image below is a blank example showing where the application status appears.
Please submit your own approved form during registration.
